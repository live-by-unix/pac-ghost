# GHOST-MAN

**GHOST-MAN** is a reverse Pac-Man browser game: you play as the ghost and chase Pac-Man around the maze.

[Play the game](https://pacghost.livebyunix.com)

## About

Instead of controlling Pac-Man, you control a red ghost. Catch Pac-Man three times before he eats every dot to win the level.

When Pac-Man collects a power pellet or enters a powered-up state, ghosts must flee and you can lose lives. Collect the red fruit for a temporary boost and a refreshed dash.

## Features

- Reverse Pac-Man gameplay
- Easy, normal, and hard difficulty modes
- Multiple AI ghosts with distinct behaviors
- Power pellets and frightened ghosts
- Dash boost with an energy cooldown
- Bonus fruit pickups
- Local high-score tracking
- Keyboard, touch, and swipe controls
- Procedurally generated arcade-style effects
- Built-in sound effects and mute controls

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | Arrow keys or WASD | Swipe |
| Dash | Space | Tap with two fingers |
| Pause | P | — |
| Mute audio | M | — |
| Choose difficulty | 1–3 on the title screen | — |
| Start / restart | Enter or any key | Tap |
